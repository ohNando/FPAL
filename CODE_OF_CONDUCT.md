# Code of conduct

FPAL is a small project run by volunteers. Everyone is welcome to report a sensor, ask a question, send a patch or point out a mistake, whatever their background or experience.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient. People who bring a sensor to test are doing us a favour.
- Disagree about the code, not about the person.
- Do not harass, insult or discriminate, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the project is discussed.
- Do not post other people's private information. This includes fingerprint template ids, serial numbers and USB traces from a machine you do not own, and your own too: they identify a real finger and a real device. Use `fpal report`, which leaves the serial number out.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. We will try to explain why.

## Reporting a problem

Use the repository's private report form: open the **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability** and start the title with "Conduct:". Only the maintainer sees it. You can also use GitHub's own "Report content" option on a comment.
